Awareness & Alignment
Process

The Awareness & Alignment™ Model is a thought-provoking, creative partnership designed to cultivate greater awareness of habituated patterns, beliefs, and conditioned responses that can inhibit you from living the most joyful and liberated life possible. Achieving without awareness often keeps us running in circles — chasing outcomes that never quite bring the stillness and peace we expected. Through the AWE Method™ and the AWExApp™, you'll develop greater clarity, alignment, and intention as you gather your own evidence about what truly matters to you. When you're no longer driven by what you think you should want, you don't just achieve — you achieve what actually matters to you.

Alignment

Begin with a complimentary 20-minute exploratory session to determine whether the AWE Method™ and the Awareness & Alignment™ Model are the right fit for your journey. If there is alignment, we'll start with a one-hour foundation session to clarify your goals, explore what may be standing in your way, and begin crafting your personalized Journey Map.

Efficient by Design

Your time (and money) matter. That's why we designed focused 40-minute coaching sessions — made possible through intentional coaching architecture and the AWExApp™. You'll arrive with clarity, drop in your focus before each session, and spend your time exploring what matters most from minute one.

Personalized Journey Map

After each session, your AWExApp™ becomes an evolving record of your journey. Continue reflecting between sessions, explore frameworks and practices tailored to your unique patterns, and co-create experiments that help transform awareness into embodied change.

Respecting the Expert in You

Awareness & Alignment Coaching™ is about you. You are the authority on your experiences, your wisdom, and what is true for you. This is your AWExpedition. Our role is to ask thoughtful questions, create space for reflection, co-create experiments, encourage curiosity, and step aside so what is already within you has room to emerge.

A Map, Not a Dependency

Our goal is to equip you with the awareness, skills, and practices to continue your journey — with or without a guide. Within ten sessions, most clients have the tools they need to continue exploring on their own. Continue with coaching, move to periodic check-ins, or fly on your own — you are the master of your travels.

Is This Right for You?

Aligned, Addition,
or Alternative

AWExpedition isn't for everyone — and that's okay. The most important thing is that you find the right support for where you are right now. Here's an honest look at where this work tends to flourish — and where something else might serve you better.

You might be a great fit

This work tends to flourish when:
  • You sense something underneath the surface but can't quite name it
  • You're tired of chasing outcomes that don't bring what you thought they would
  • You're at a crossroads — in career, identity, relationships, or simply your relationship with yourself
  • You're curious and reflective, willing to sit with what comes up and explore it during the week
  • You're ready to loosen your grip on control and open to where the path might lead. The door doesn't have to be wide open. Just not welded shut.

A natural addition

A&A Coaching pairs well with:
  • Therapy or counseling — we're forward-focused and strengths-based, a natural complement to deeper clinical work
  • Energy work or other holistic practices
  • Executive or career coaching where the inner landscape matters as much as the outer goal

An alternative may serve better

This might not be the right fit right now if:
  • Life feels so heavy that what you need most is space to be heard and held — a therapist, counselor or energy healer may be the better fit
  • You are struggling with a medical mental health issue and are taking prescribed medication — work with your psychiatrist to determine the best course of action
  • The between-session work feels out of reach — the AWExApp™ is central to your journey and the between-session work is where the magic happens. If your schedule doesn't allow for practice in between sessions, this might not be the right season for your AWExpedition — we want you to get the most from your journey.
  • You're looking for a more directive experience — someone to assess, prescribe, and hand you a plan. That's a legitimate need, just a different kind of guide.
